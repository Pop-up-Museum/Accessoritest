Arm your deck.

A vault for three decks whose magnetic lid unfolds into a battlefield,
or comes away whole to be played anywhere.

Mediolani · Anno MMXXVI

Caput primumOf the weapon

Closed, it is a case of rigid boards wrapped in leather. Its lid is the battlefield: six panels that unfold beside three decks of a hundred cards.

The field is held to the vault by magnets alone. Leave it attached and play beside your decks, or lift it away whole: it becomes a free playmat of seventy centimetres. When the game ends, it snaps back and folds around the decks like armour.

  • Field700 × 370 mm
  • Panelssix, rigid
  • Decks3 × 100 sleeved
  • Fieldmagnetic, detachable
  • Magnets16

Caput secundumOf its opening

  1. I. The flank lifts.
  2. II. The ranks unfold.
  3. III. The field swings over the vault.
  4. IV. Battle may begin.
  5. V. Or lift the field away: it is held by magnets alone.

And to close it again, four moves drawn to scale. Letters name the panels from the vault outward. Panel A meets the vault on magnets, so the whole field can be lifted away at any moment.

Caput tertiumOf the four kingdoms

Four realms shared the world before the Auripietra broke its balance. Choose one on the map, and the book will open on its kingdom and its vault.

Hover a kingdom to read its creed.

Or break the seal and see the four banners side by side.

Metal · North-west · Kingdom I

Empire of Vaporonia

“Flesh fails. Metal endures.”

The empire that beat the cold with fire. Since the Auripietra was found, Vaporonia burns day and night. Its king, Ludovico the Prometheus, no longer has a body of flesh.

Here flesh is a flaw to be corrected. At eighteen every subject faces the Hunt and walks into the snow inside steam-driven armour.
